This repository hosts my personal academic portfolio and website, showcasing my research as a bioinformatician and geneticist at the Centro Andaluz de Biología Molecular y Medicina Regenerativa (CABIMER-CSIC) in Sevilla, Spain.

## About

I am a bioinformatician and geneticist specialized in the genetic architecture of rare diseases and cancer evolution. My research focuses on identifying genetic and epigenetic determinants underlying disease mechanisms, with particular emphasis on:

- **Rare Disease Genomics**: Investigating the genetic basis of Meniere's disease, tinnitus, and vestibular disorders
- **Cancer Epigenetics**: Studying epigenetic stress resistance mechanisms in cancer (MOMENTUM Programme)
- **Computational Genetics**: Developing and applying bioinformatics approaches for variant analysis and disease gene discovery

## Research Highlights

My work spans multiple areas of human genetics and genomics:

- **Meniere's Disease**: Identified rare variants in genes such as *OTOG*, *GJD3*, and *TECTA* associated with familial and sporadic forms
- **Tinnitus Genetics**: Characterized coding and non-coding rare variants in synaptic and neuronal genes (*ANK2*) contributing to severe tinnitus
- **Clinical Trials**: Statistical analysis and coordination for the UNITI-RCT international trial on tinnitus treatments

## Publications

My research has been published in high-impact scientific journals including *Nature Communications*, *Genome Medicine*, *npJ Genomic Medicine*, *Human Genetics*, and *EBioMedicine*. For a complete list of publications, please visit the [Publications section](https://algalliard.github.io/publications/) of my website.

## Building and Running

This website is built using the [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio) Jekyll theme. To run locally:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/algalliard/algalliard.github.io.git

# Install dependencies
bundle install

# Serve locally
bundle exec jekyll serve
```

The site will be available at `http://localhost:4000`.
